    Know your enemies

    Face six different bosses at levels 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30. Gain five more levels after defeating the sixth boss, and all six return together for the final boss encounter. Defeat them all and continue surviving without further bosses. Lure and Time Freeze cannot affect bosses; survival time, scrolling and the red zone remain stopped until the arena is clear. Later bosses drop much larger seed jackpots, more coins, stronger weapon upgrades and more mystery boxes.
